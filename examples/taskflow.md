# TaskFlow

**An AI to-do app that plans your day for you.**

TaskFlow is a productivity app for busy knowledge workers. Instead of just holding a
list of tasks, it looks at your calendar, your deadlines, and how long things usually
take you, and automatically builds a realistic schedule for your day — slotting each
task into a time block. When meetings move or a task runs long, it re-plans the rest
of your day automatically.

## How it works
- Connect your Google or Outlook calendar.
- Add tasks with a rough estimate (or let TaskFlow guess from past tasks).
- TaskFlow auto-schedules everything into open time blocks around your meetings.
- A daily "what changed" summary shows what got bumped and why.

## Plans
- **Free:** up to 15 active tasks, daily auto-plan, calendar sync for one calendar.
- **Pro ($8/month):** unlimited tasks, multi-calendar, automatic re-planning when your
  day changes, focus-time protection, and weekly review analytics.
- Every new user gets a 14-day Pro trial.

## Who it's for
People who have more to do than time, who live in their calendar, and who lose time
just deciding what to work on next. It is *not* a team project-management tool — it's
personal, for individuals.
